01
CHARACTER SALES

ADOPTS

♡
01 How do adopts work? +

Each adopt will have its own rules, price and payment information. Please read the individual adopt description carefully before claiming.

Once payment has been received, the character and any included files will be transferred to the new owner.

02 Can I resell an adopt? +

Reselling rules may vary depending on the adopt. Please check the specific character's terms before reselling.

Do not sell a character for more than you originally paid unless additional commissioned artwork is included in the new price.

03 Can I change the design? +

Small design changes are generally fine, but please keep the original character recognizable unless the adopt's rules say otherwise.

04 Do adopts come with commercial rights? +

Commercial rights are not automatically included unless specifically stated. Please contact me if you want to use an adopt for commercial purposes.

02
PERSONAL ARTWORK

COMMISSIONS

✦
01 What do you draw? +

I mainly draw female characters, OCs, fanart, armor and stylized character illustrations.

Please check my commission page for examples of my current style and available options.

02 How long do commissions take? +

The estimated turnaround depends on the complexity of the commission and my current queue.

I will give you an approximate timeframe before starting your commission.

03 Can I request changes? +

Yes! Minor corrections are possible during the sketch stage. Major changes requested after the sketch has been approved may require an additional fee.

04 Can I use my commission as a profile picture? +

Personal use such as profile pictures, wallpapers and social media posts is fine unless otherwise specified.

Please credit me when possible.

05 Do you draw NSFW? +

NSFW commissions are available depending on the type of request. Feel free to contact me privately with details so I can confirm whether I can take the commission.

03
BUSINESS & LICENSING

COMMERCIAL

◇
01 Do you accept commercial commissions? +

Yes! Commercial projects can include illustrations, character art, promotional artwork, covers and other creative work.

Commercial pricing is separate from my personal commission prices and depends on the scope of the project and intended use.

02 What counts as commercial use? +

Commercial use includes artwork used to directly promote, sell or generate revenue for a product, business, game, book, brand, merchandise or other paid project.

03 Can I use commissioned art on merchandise? +

Merchandise use requires commercial rights. Please tell me what kind of merchandise you are planning before ordering.

04 Can you work on game projects? +

Yes! I'm open to discussing game-related projects, including character art, illustrations, promotional materials and visual assets.

04
LET'S CREATE TOGETHER

COLLABS

♡
01 Are you open for collaborations? +

I'm open to selected collaborations, zines, art projects and creative events that fit my interests and schedule.

02 Can I invite you to a zine? +

Absolutely! Please send me the zine name, theme, schedule, requirements and any important information when contacting me.

03 Do you accept unpaid collaborations? +

This depends on the project. I'm happy to consider personal and community projects, but please provide enough information for me to understand the scope and expectations.

04 How should I contact you about a project? +

Please send a short introduction together with the project name, your role, deadlines, requirements and what you would like me to do.

The more information you provide, the easier it is for me to give you an answer!

05
ORDERS & TRANSACTIONS

PAYMENT

$
01 What currency are your prices in? +

All prices listed on my website are in USD.

02 When do I pay? +

Payment is normally required before I begin working on the final artwork. I will confirm the total price and details before sending the invoice.

03 Do you offer payment plans? +

Payment plans may be available for larger commissions or character sheets. Please ask before placing the order.

04 Are refunds available? +

Refunds depend on the stage of the commission. Once significant work has been completed, a full refund may no longer be possible.

Please contact me as soon as possible if something goes wrong with your order.

05 What payment methods do you accept? +

Payment methods and available options may vary depending on the commission. I will provide the available payment method when confirming your order.
